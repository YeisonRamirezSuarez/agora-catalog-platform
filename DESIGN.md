# Design System: Agorá Catalog

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Table Set at Night"**

Every public catalog is a neighborhood shop's window told as a table laid out after dark: full-bleed photography under a warm night gradient with a large serif headline in two tones, and below it a cream counter where each product is a paper card that adds itself to an order. The order leaves through WhatsApp with quantities and total already written. The world has three materials (night, cream, paper), three voices (Playfair Display for what the shop says, Ms Madi for what the shop scribbles in the margin, Onest for everything the shopper reads to act), and two business colors that each store sets for itself: a dark primary that fills every action button and a light accent that carries the gold italic, the hero button, and the stamp-like details.

The admin, super admin, login, and "Catálogo no disponible" screens belong to Agorá Catalog, not to the store: white surfaces on a warm paper wash, the seal's warm ink for selection and dark buttons, the seal's gold on the selected menu icon, a dark-gold focus ring, Playfair Display 600 for page titles only, and Onest for everything else. The router marks them with `body[data-surface=panel]`, so the storefront never inherits these values. They keep the operational layout and components intact and meet the storefront only inside the store preview in Configuración, which renders the night cover, the gold italic, and the cream strip in miniature.

The storefront follows the client's comp (`Salsamentaria Javier_ Tradición y Buen Sabor.png`) by explicit user direction; where this document and the comp disagree, the build and the comp win.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Two grounds: night (#15120D family) for photography bands, header, and footer; cream for the shopping counter.
- Products on warm paper cards with a hairline ring, a 4:3 photo, a crimson price, and a quantity stepper beside a dark cart button.
- Playfair Display headlines with the second line in gold italic; Ms Madi only for asides rotated a few degrees off the grid.
- Wave edges where night meets cream; a circular stamp and a pinned paper tag as the shop's marks.
- One action color per store (the business primary), validated to carry white text.

## Colors

A warm night-and-cream palette where the store's own two colors do the talking, crimson carries money, and three soft badge tints mark the merchandising.

### Primary
- **Business Primary** (`business-primary`, Javier's default, configurable per store as `--brand`): fills every action that moves toward an order: the cart button, "Enviar pedido", the catalog search submit, the active list/grid toggle, the help card, the WhatsApp contact button, and the store focus ring. Configuración rejects any value below 4.5:1 against white.

### Secondary
- **Business Accent** (`business-accent`, Javier's default gold, configurable as `--accent`) with **On Accent** (`on-accent`) for text on it: the italic second line of every hero headline, the hero "Explorar el catálogo" button, the plan arrow buttons, the sellos icons, the focus ring on night, and a darkened mix (62% accent into #3A2706) for handwritten notes on cream. Configuración rejects any value below 4.5:1 against night.

### Tertiary
- **Price Crimson** (`price`): prices, the order line subtotals, the cart count bubble, and a saved favorite heart.
- **Badge tints**: Más vendido honey (`badge-best` / `badge-best-ink`), Promo yellow (`badge-promo` / `badge-promo-ink`), Nuevo mint (`badge-new` / `badge-new-ink`). Agotado uses cream ink with white text.

### Neutral
- **Night** (`night`): hero, header when solid (94% alpha with 10px blur), combos band, contact photo card, catalog hero, footer, and the theme color. Photography sits under gradients built from this value.
- **Night Text** (`night-text`) and **Night Muted** (`night-muted`): subtitles on night, and the header's secondary labels ("Mi pedido").
- **Cream** (`cream`): the counter ground for every storefront section below the hero.
- **Sand** (`sand`): plan cards and photo placeholders.
- **Paper** (`paper`): product cards, filter card, chips, sheets and the order drawer.
- **Cream Ink / Ink Soft / Muted / Line** (`cream-ink`, `cream-ink-soft`, `cream-muted`, `cream-line`): text, secondary text, struck prices and hints, and hairlines on the storefront.
- **Admin neutrals** (`ink`, `ink-hover`, `ink-soft`, `muted`, `faint`, `line`, `line-strong`, `wash`, `white`, `switch-off`): the panel's warm neutrals taken from the Agorá seal (ink #1C1814, gold #E0A43A); `panel-focus` (dark gold, 5.5:1 on white) is the panel's focus ring and input halo; `agora-gold` only marks the selected menu icon on ink (8:1).
- **Admin status** (`deal`/`deal-ink` for the offer chip in the products table; `whatsapp`/`whatsapp-press` for "Guardar precios"; `danger`, `ok`, `warn` with their soft and ink pairs for validation, import reports, and switches).

### Named Rules
**The Two Grounds Rule.** The storefront has exactly two grounds: night for photography and frame (header, hero, band, footer), cream for shopping. Paper sits on cream; nothing sits on white except fields and steppers.

**The One Action Color Rule.** Buttons that advance an order fill the business primary with white text. Gold fills only the hero button and the plan arrows; crimson never fills a button.

**The Crimson Means Money Rule.** Crimson marks a price, the order count, and a saved favorite. Nothing else is crimson.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Playfair Display (with Georgia, Times New Roman), roman 600 to 700 and italic
**Script Font:** Ms Madi (cursive), weight 400
**Body Font:** Onest (with system-ui, -apple-system, Segoe UI, Roboto)

**Character:** A high-contrast transitional serif speaks for the shop in large sizes, the second line turning to gold italic; a quick handwritten script adds the owner's margin notes; a friendly grotesque handles every price, button, field, and list so nothing functional depends on the decorative faces.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (Playfair 600, fluid 64 to 84px desktop, 40 to 50px mobile, 1 to 1.04): the hero headline, roman line in white and accent line in gold italic. The catalog hero uses the lower endpoints (64px / 40px).
- **Headline** (Playfair 600, 68px desktop / 44px mobile): "Combos de la casa" on the band. The contact photo card ("Nos vemos en Javier.") runs 58px / 40px.
- **Section** (Playfair 700, 48px desktop / 30px mobile, 1.1, centered): section titles such as "¿Cuál es el plan?".
- **Title serif** (Playfair 700, 28px and 26px): sheet titles (product, filters, Mi pedido, info), plan card names on desktop, the mobile menu links. Plan names on mobile run 20px; the store name in the header runs 30px desktop / 23px mobile over a 15px / 12.5px "Salsamentaria".
- **Notes** (Ms Madi 400, 56px favorites heading on desktop at 0.84 line height; 40px side and contact notes; 34px and 26px paper tag and mobile notes; hero note 30px): always rotated 6 to 10 degrees; the mobile favorites heading falls back to Playfair 700 at 24px.
- **Title** (Onest 800 at 22px for sheet and admin form titles; 700 at 17px for totals and empty-state headings).
- **Price** (Onest 800, 18px, -0.01em, crimson; 26px in the product sheet): former price struck in cream muted at 13px weight 500.
- **Body** (Onest 400, 15px, 1.45): default; product sheet descriptions run 1.55 in cream ink soft.
- **Body large** (Onest 400, 16px, 1.5) sets subtitles on night (18px desktop, 34 to 40ch measure) and full-width sheet button text at weight 700.
- **Item name** (Onest 700, 15px, 1.25): product card names. Descriptions are 13px muted, clamped to two lines.
- **Label** (Onest 600, 14px) and **Meta** (Onest 400, 13px, 1.4).
- **Badge** (Onest 700, 12px) and **Nav micro** (Onest 500, 11px, the admin's mobile tab bar labels).

### Named Rules
**The Script Is an Aside Rule.** Ms Madi never carries information the shopper needs to act. Notes and the paper tag are `aria-hidden` restatements; prices, names, buttons, and fields are always Onest.

**The Two-Tone Headline Rule.** A hero headline is a roman line and an italic line in the business accent. The italic is the emphasis; no other color or weight change is added inside a headline.

**The Tabular Columns Rule.** `tnum` applies where numbers update in place or stack: the header total, the order total, quantity outputs, the price range, admin price inputs and tables. Catalog card prices stay proportional.

## Layout

Mobile first. The storefront header is fixed, 68px tall (84px from 960px), transparent over the hero and solid night once scrolled. The hero fills min(620px, 100svh) on mobile with the headline anchored low and a search field inside it; from 960px it is min(800px, 96vh), left-aligned in a 1240px frame, with the hero button, the handwritten note with its arrow, the circular stamp at bottom right, and a four-column sellos row overlapping the photo's foot.

Cream sections follow with a wave edge (27px mobile, 40px desktop) cut from the cream into the night above. Mobile shows category circles (64px photos), then "¿Cuál es el plan?" as a horizontal snap rail of 310px plan cards, then favorites as a two-up snap rail. From 960px plans become a three-column grid in a 1180px frame, and favorites a 1240px rail of 208 to 250px cards with category chips and arrow buttons between the script heading and "Ver todo". The combos band (440px mobile, 500px desktop) carries a mirrored wave at its top. The contact section stacks a photo card over a cream sheet that overlaps it by 26px with 24px top corners; from 960px it becomes two columns (1 : 1.05) 64px apart.

The catalog page opens with a night hero, then a cream sheet pulled up 32px (44px desktop) with 24px top corners. Mobile: search across the full width, sort select and a "Filtros" button that opens a sheet, a two-column grid 10px apart. From 960px: a 250px sticky filter column beside an auto-fill grid of 210px minimum cards 18px apart, plus a grid/list toggle.

Sheets rise from below on mobile and center as dialogs on desktop; the order ("Mi pedido") is a 440px right drawer on desktop. There is no mobile bottom bar: the order lives in the header's cart button.

The admin shell is a 248px white sidebar beside a wash ground with a 1180px main column (32px top, 36px sides); at 860px the sidebar becomes a fixed bottom tab bar. Forms are two columns (three for prices), one column at 640px; the product editor pairs a 340px sticky photo column until 1000px.

Breakpoints observed: 640px, 760px, 860px, 960px, 1000px.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth on the storefront is photographic and tonal: night gradients over photos, paper over cream, and the wave edges. Product and filter cards carry a hairline ring shadow instead of a border; shadows otherwise appear on things that float (plan cards on hover, category circles, the paper tag, sheets, the admin save bar). Hearts over photos use a soft drop-shadow so the outline reads on any image. No hard offset shadows anywhere.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Card ring** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px rgba(33,28,22,.06), 0 2px 8px -3px rgba(33,28,22,.12)`): product cards at rest; the filter card takes the ring alone.
- **Plan lift** (`box-shadow: 0 14px 30px -16px rgba(33,28,22,.5)`): plan cards on hover, with a 2px rise.
- **Circle** (`box-shadow: 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(33,28,22,.35)`): category circle photos.
- **Paper tag** (`box-shadow: 0 10px 22px -10px rgba(0,0,0,.6)`): the pinned note on the combos band.
- **Rest / Float / Sheet** (`--sh-1`, `--sh-2`, `--sh-3`): admin round buttons and login card; logos and the save bar; sheets.

### Named Rules
**The Ring Not Border Rule.** Storefront cards on cream take the 1px ring shadow, not a drawn border. Fields, steppers, and chips are the only bordered things on the counter.

## Shapes

Soft corners scaled by size: 2px for underlines and the range track, 3px for the paper tag, 6px admin badges, 8px for the hero button, 10px for steppers, cart buttons and order thumbnails, 12px for fields, selects and the contact button, 14px for product cards and full-width sheet buttons, 16px for plan and filter cards, 22px for sheets, 24px for the cream sheets that overlap night (contact, catalog) and the desktop contact photo. Badges and filter chips are pills. Category photos, header logos, icon buttons, hearts, and plan arrows are full circles; admin logos keep the 26% squircle. Wave edges are the only non-rectangular silhouettes, plus the rotated paper tag and the circular stamp with text set on its ring.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Hero search (mobile):** 50px paper field, 10px radius, 3px accent ring on focus.
- **Catalog search:** white, cream-line border, 12px radius, 48px tall, with an attached 52px business primary submit; on focus the border turns primary with an 18% primary halo.
- **Quantity stepper:** white, cream-line border, 10px radius, 40px tall (52px in the product sheet, 38px in order lines), tabular count.
- **Admin fields:** white, line-strong border, 12px radius, 44px; focus turns ink with a 16% dark-gold halo.
- **Touch screens (`pointer:coarse`):** every text field, select and textarea renders at 16px, because iPhone Safari zooms the page into any field under 16px. `touch-action:manipulation` on `html` stops double-tap zoom on fast taps (the + and − steppers); pinch zoom still works. Never use `maximum-scale` or `user-scalable=no` for this.

### Navigation
- **Header:** round logo (44px mobile, 58px desktop, white ring padding around the emblem), the two-line serif lockup, desktop links in night text at 15px weight 500 with a 2px white underline for the current page, search and the cart button with its crimson count and "Mi pedido $total". - **Admin:** 248px sidebar with ink selection; bottom tab bar at 860px and below.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** fill every button that advances an order with the business primary and white text, and keep Configuración's 4.5:1 checks for both business colors.
- **Do** set products on paper cards over cream with the card ring, a 4:3 photo, a crimson Onest price, and the stepper beside the cart button.
- **Do** write each hero headline as a roman line plus a gold italic line.
- **Do** join night and cream with the wave edge.
- **Do** keep handwritten notes rotated, `aria-hidden`, and redundant with nearby Onest text.
- **Do** keep test photography credited (`docs/prototipo/img/CREDITOS.md`) and never present it as the business's own.

### Don't:
- **Don't** set prices, product names, buttons, or form text in Playfair Display or Ms Madi.
- **Don't** use crimson for anything but prices, the order count, and a saved favorite.
- **Don't** fill buttons with gold beyond the hero CTA and the plan arrows.
- **Don't** add a mobile bottom bar; the order lives in the header.
- **Don't** bring cream, night, Ms Madi, or the store's business colors into the admin panel outside the store preview; Playfair Display appears there only in page titles.
- **Don't** reuse the tracked-caps line above the hero and catalog headlines on new surfaces; it is carried here only to match the client comp.
